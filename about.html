About SG Enterprises

SG Enterprises is a technology company dedicated to empowering voters and citizens through innovative mobile applications. Founded with a vision to enhance democratic participation, we develop tools that provide accurate, timely, and reliable information.

Our flagship product, Janmat, is designed to bridge the gap between candidates and voters, ensuring transparency and informed decision-making in the electoral process.

Our Mission

To create a connected ecosystem where voters have direct access to candidate information and can engage in meaningful dialogues, fostering a more informed and participative democracy.
